# Customer Complaint Triage and Response Application

## Overview

Financial institutions receive large volumes of customer complaints that must be reviewed, categorized, investigated, and responded to in a timely and consistent manner. Manual complaint processing can be labor-intensive and may result in inconsistent classifications or responses.

This project develops an AI-assisted complaint triage pipeline that processes raw customer complaint narratives and generates structured, actionable outputs. The system classifies complaints, summarizes narratives, and drafts initial responses while emphasizing explainability, consistency, and auditability.

The solution is designed with regulatory environments in mind, where complaint handling practices can impact compliance obligations, operational risk, and customer trust.

## Business Problem

Poor complaint management can expose organizations to:

- Regulatory scrutiny
- Compliance violations
- Reputational damage
- Customer dissatisfaction
- Operational inefficiencies

Financial institutions require scalable processes capable of maintaining consistency while supporting human review and oversight.

## Project Objectives

- Classify customer complaints into meaningful categories
- Summarize complaint narratives into concise descriptions
- Generate draft responses aligned with institutional tone
- Improve consistency across complaint handling workflows
- Support explainable and auditable AI-assisted decision making

## Technologies Used

- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Regular Expressions (Regex)
- Natural Language Processing (NLP)

## Methodology

### Data Preparation

- Text cleaning
- Normalization
- Removal of irrelevant characters and formatting

### Complaint Classification

Complaint narratives are analyzed and categorized into predefined issue types to support triage workflows.

### Narrative Summarization

Long complaint descriptions are condensed into concise summaries to improve analyst efficiency.

### Response Generation

The system drafts initial responses that can be reviewed and modified by compliance or customer service teams.

### Explainability Focus

Outputs are designed to be:

- Transparent
- Reviewable
- Consistent
- Auditable

## Results

The project demonstrates how AI-assisted complaint processing can reduce manual effort while improving consistency and supporting regulatory compliance objectives.
